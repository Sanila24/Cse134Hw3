Why I Enjoy Fragrances

I’m a big believer in good hygiene and smelling good. For me, wearing a fragrance adds a boost of confidence that carries into the rest of my day.

How My Collection Started

My first-ever cologne was Dior Sauvage. That bottle was the beginning of an interest that has grown into a collection of around 10–15 bottles.

Since then, my taste has shifted toward niche fragrances and high-end designer colognes. My collection has expanded quite a bit from where it started.

My Current Favorite

Louis Vuitton Imagination

My favorite fragrance right now is Louis Vuitton Imagination. It’s my daily driver, meaning it’s the one I reach for regularly for everyday wear.
