Where It Started

When the pandemic started, I wanted to do something active instead of just sitting around at home. I began working out in my backyard with at-home workouts.

Eventually, I got a gym membership and started training consistently. What began as a way to stay busy at home became a regular part of my life.

What Keeps Me Coming Back

A quick workout is a great way for me to take my mind off whatever is going on in my life. It gives me a break and helps me feel more confident.

Most importantly, working out helps me stay healthy, both mentally and physically. That’s what makes it such an important part of my routine.

My Favorite Workout

Arms

Arms are my favorite muscle group to train. While I enjoy the workout itself, the confidence boost and mental break afterward are just as valuable to me.
