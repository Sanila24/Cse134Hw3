A Lifelong Sports Fan

I’ve been a huge sports fan my whole life. I watch pretty much every sport, but my favorites are soccer, basketball, and football. If I’m watching something on TV, my phone, or my laptop outside of schoolwork, it’s almost definitely sports-related.

My Soccer Background

Soccer has been a big part of my life beyond just watching it. I played competitively throughout my childhood until I was 18 years old.

Although I no longer play competitively, I still try to get out and play whenever I can.

The Teams I Cheer For

Real Madrid

Cristiano Ronaldo was my favorite player growing up, and he’s a big reason I became a Real Madrid fan. They remain my favorite soccer team.

Los Angeles Lakers

I’m also a big Lakers fan because of Kobe Bryant. He was the player who drew me to the team. Rest in peace, Kobe.

Los Angeles Rams

The Rams are my favorite football team, but my support also extends to any player who happens to be on my fantasy team.

Talk Sports With Me

Whether it’s soccer, basketball, football, or fantasy sports, I’m always interested in a conversation. Get in touch.
