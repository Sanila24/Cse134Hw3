Making Mealtime Easier

DineRoute is an AI dining recommender that helps UCSD students decide where and what to eat. It compares options across seven dining halls based on personal preferences, dietary restrictions, and nutrition goals.

Why I Built It

Choosing a meal can mean checking several menus, especially when you’re looking for something that fits your diet or a specific craving. I built DineRoute to bring those choices together and make that decision easier.

What Can You Do With It?

  • Describe what you feel like eating.
  • Include dietary restrictions and nutrition goals.
  • Get a suggested dining hall and meal.
  • Compare alternative dining hall options.

An Example

You could ask for a high-protein dinner with around 800 calories, no pork, and something spicy. DineRoute uses those preferences to suggest a meal.

Try It Out

The hosted demo uses saved menu data, so its suggestions may not reflect today’s dining hall menus.
